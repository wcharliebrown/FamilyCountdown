# Privacy Policy — FamilyCountdown

_Last updated: July 31, 2026_

FamilyCountdown does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no account, no sign-in, and no server. Every event you
add — its name, date, and settings — is stored **only on your device**, in the
app's local storage. It never leaves your iPad.

## Network use

The app makes no network requests for its own purposes. It does not contact
any server operated by us or by any third party, and it contains no analytics,
advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

## Data sharing

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or disclose.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
